Eventbrite
Eventbrite is a mobile app and online platform for discovering, creating, promoting, and managing events while handling ticket sales and registrations. It is widely used by organizers ranging from individuals and nonprofits to businesses hosting conferences, workshops, concerts, community gatherings, and virtual events, with availability across many countries.

Core features
Event organizers can create event pages, set up free or paid tickets, manage attendee registrations, issue digital tickets, and monitor sales through built-in analytics. The mobile app also allows attendees to browse local events, purchase tickets, store digital tickets, receive event reminders, and check in at venues using QR codes.

What makes it notable
A key strength of Eventbrite is its combination of event discovery and ticketing in a single ecosystem. Organizers can promote events through social media and email integrations, while attendees can search by location, date, category, or interest. The platform supports both in-person and virtual events and provides tools for refunds, waitlists, promotional codes, and capacity management.

Typical use cases
The app serves a broad range of events, including live entertainment, professional conferences, classes, fundraisers, networking meetups, sports activities, festivals, and community gatherings. Its self-service design makes it suitable for both first-time organizers planning small local events and organizations managing larger ticketed experiences.
